Case Viability Screening — Pre-Expert Medical Record Assessment

A focused physician screen to determine whether the medical record supports the case theory before larger expert or litigation spend.

Starting scopeFrom $350
Turnaround72 hours
DeliverablePre-expert viability memo
Founder’s Advantage

Medisprudence applies physician review logic shaped by payer-side utilization review: documentation thresholds, medical necessity, causation support, treatment consistency, and criteria application.

What this review answers

  • Does the treatment record medically support the claimed injury or complication?
  • Where will causation, necessity, or damages likely be attacked?
  • Is this case worth deeper medical workup?
  • What records or clarifications are needed before expert retention?

Best used when

  • Early case evaluation before expert spend.
  • High-volume intake where counsel needs triage.
  • Cases with unclear causation or inconsistent documentation.

What you receive

Strength/weakness snapshot
Causation and documentation gap notes
Medical records needed list
Preliminary defense vulnerability map
Recommendation: proceed, clarify, or decline/escalate

## Accuracy and positioning corrections

- Removed universal claims that fees are recoverable at settlement.
- Reframed Daubert/admissibility statements as clinical methodology and foundation issues for counsel and retained experts.
- Corrected the inaccurate conflation of six benefit classifications with six NQTL categories.
- Removed implied certainty about an opponent’s exact strategy.
- Reframed bellwether work as pilot-led and capacity-validated rather than unlimited-volume physician review.
- Clarified that medical reserve work is a clinical input, not actuarial reserve certification.
- Clarified that MHPAEA work is a counsel-directed clinical workstream, not a complete legal compliance certification.
- Clarified lawful criteria access and non-use of former-employer confidential information.

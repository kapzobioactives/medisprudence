MDL Defense Service

Bellwether Defense Medical Screening

For MDL defense and mass tort defense coordination — physician evaluation of plaintiff medical claims at volume under consistent criteria, calibrated to the framework that will govern Daubert challenges.

Defense / mass-tort serviceScoped fixed feeBatch review option
Custom pricing · Volume arrangements
Founder's Advantage

When defense liaison counsel needs to assess the strength of the plaintiff pool's medical claims, a physician who can evaluate records at volume under the same criteria framework that will govern Daubert challenges is exactly what they need. Our physician's payer-side experience — evaluating claims at volume under consistent criteria — translates directly to MDL defense medical coordination.

What the screening covers

Per-plaintiff medical claim evaluation using a consistent analytical framework — identifying which plaintiff records present the strongest medical cases, which are most vulnerable to defense medical challenges, and where Daubert vulnerabilities exist across the plaintiff pool.

The same bellwether screening methodology available to plaintiff liaison counsel, applied for defense coordination. Custom scope based on plaintiff pool size and case type.

Engagement structure

Custom scope: Defined by plaintiff pool size, case type, and defense coordination requirements.

Volume pricing: Per-record rates for large plaintiff pools. Monthly capacity arrangements available.

Institutional terms: Net 30 invoice terms. Retainer arrangements for ongoing defense coordination.

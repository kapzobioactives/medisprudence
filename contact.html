Before you write

For case review: Use the Request Case Review form — it gathers the right information without requiring PHI upfront.

For scope questions: Describe the case type, the injury, the decision you need to make, and your deadline. That is enough to get a pricing and turnaround confirmation.

For white-label inquiry: Describe your firm, your attorney client volume, and the services where you currently refer out or turn down physician-level analysis requests.

For bad faith cases: Mention the denial type, the criteria system referenced in the denial letter (InterQual, MCG, or proprietary), and whether you have the peer-to-peer review documentation.

Why the case cost framing matters

In a contingency-fee practice, Medisprudence fees advance as case costs — the same cost category used for expert consultation, medical record retrieval, and investigation. The entire engagement appears as one line item on the case ledger, one invoice to file, and is recovered from settlement proceeds. No new budget category. No separate approval for each service. No decision at each stage of the case.

How firms may account for the engagement

Medisprudence issues one invoice for the scoped engagement. Some plaintiff firms may classify that invoice as a litigation case cost, but treatment of the expense depends on the firm’s client agreement, governing law, ethics rules, and the case outcome. The operational benefit is one scope, one approval, and one invoice instead of separate procurement at each stage.

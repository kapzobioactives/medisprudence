Partnership Program

White-Label Physician Intelligence for LNC Firms

Physician-authored case intelligence delivered under your brand — for the cases where nurse review reaches its ceiling and your attorney clients need MBBS-level interpretation.

Custom pricing · Contact for details
Partnership value

The LNC firm retains the client relationship and record-management role while Medisprudence supplies a defined physician interpretation layer. The partnership is built around accurate authorship, conflict screening, source-linked analysis, secure transfer, and agreed scope—not a claim that nursing and physician functions are interchangeable.

How the partnership works

  • 01
    Your brand, our physician

    Deliverables are produced under your firm’s brand and formatting. Your attorney clients see your name. The physician intelligence comes from Medisprudence.

  • 02
    Monthly capacity retainer

    High-volume LNC firms can engage on a monthly retainer — a fixed number of physician-review deliverables per month at wholesale rates. One monthly invoice, predictable overhead, no per-case procurement decision. Retainer tiers available from 4 deliverables/month. Contact for pricing.

  • 03
    Per-case referral arrangement

    For firms that prefer flexibility over volume commitment — Medisprudence accepts per-case referrals and direct referrals with attribution at wholesale rates.

  • 04
    Wholesale pricing

    Partner pricing is below retail rates, allowing LNC firms to mark up physician intelligence appropriately while remaining competitive.

  • 05
    Conflict management

    Medisprudence conducts conflict checks at intake and will not serve both sides of the same matter.

When your clients need physician-level review

Cases where causation requires physician-level assessment of mechanism vs. pathology — beyond what nurse review can authoritatively address.

IME reports that require a physician to identify clinical weaknesses and generate precise deposition questions.

Pre-expert screening where the attorney needs physician judgment on case viability — not nurse-level summary.

Defense Medical Lens requests where a physician must reconstruct the defense medical theory from first-hand application of payer-review methodology.

A structured physician layer for LNC practices

Extend the LNC firm’s capabilities without surrendering the client relationship

White-label physician intelligence is a delivery partnership, not a separate clinical method. The LNC firm continues to own the attorney relationship while Medisprudence supplies a defined physician-review component under agreed authorship, branding, conflict, security, and quality terms.

Legal nurse consultants are often best positioned to organize records, develop chronologies, and understand the attorney’s workflow. Some matters nevertheless require physician interpretation of causation, treatment necessity, adverse reports, disability evidence, or utilization-management logic. Building that capacity case by case through informal referrals can create inconsistent fees, turnaround, and output.

The partnership establishes a repeatable catalog, intake protocol, scope boundary, revision process, and service-level expectation. It can be co-branded, partner-branded, or delivered with disclosed physician authorship depending on the engagement and applicable professional obligations.

Review method

A four-stage partner workflow

01

Partner intake and conflict screen

The LNC firm sends non-PHI case facts first so fit, conflicts, timing, and the proposed physician role can be checked before records are accepted.

02

Scope, authorship, and credit assignment

The parties agree the question, deliverable, client-facing role, branding, disclosure, fee, and deadline in writing for each matter or protocol.

03

Secure physician review and quality control

Records move through the agreed secure channel; findings are source-linked and checked for scope, limitations, and consistency before release.

04

Partner-controlled delivery

The LNC firm manages client communication and presentation while preserving the accuracy of physician conclusions, authorship, and professional limits.

Inputs

Terms that should be settled before the first case

A sustainable white-label relationship depends more on clear operating terms than on a headline discount.

  • Service catalog, page/record limits, turnaround, and revision rounds
  • Conflict-check identifiers and non-circumvention expectations
  • Branding, authorship, reliance, and client-disclosure requirements
  • BAA/data-processing terms and permitted secure transfer methods
  • Retainer credits, overflow pricing, rush work, and unused-capacity rules
Deliverable

What the partner receives

The clinical product remains source-linked and scope-specific even when the visual presentation is adapted to the partner’s practice.

  • Defined physician-authored or physician-directed deliverable
  • Partner-approved format and terminology
  • Source references and limitation statements
  • Predictable status communication and delivery process
  • Optional recurring capacity arrangement after workflow validation
Service fit

Where this fits among physician-capacity options

The partnership model is intended for LNC firms that want a repeatable physician-review layer while retaining control of client communication, pricing, and presentation under agreed authorship terms.

Informal physician network

Useful for occasional referrals, but quality, turnaround, conflicts, and pricing may vary from matter to matter. A defined partner workflow creates repeatable intake and quality controls.

Direct referral to a physician reviewer

May solve the immediate need, but shifts the client relationship outside the LNC firm. White-label work keeps partner communication and delivery roles defined in advance.

Physician employee or general outsourcing

A physician hire creates fixed overhead; general outsourcing adds capacity but may not add physician authorship. The partner model is scoped to physician review when volume does not justify a full-time role.

Partnership mechanics: Branding, authorship, confidentiality, non-circumvention, communication roles, and permissible editing are agreed before the first record transfer—not inferred after delivery.
Scope discipline

Best-fit partner profile

Designed to support

  • LNC firms with recurring attorney demand for physician interpretation.
  • Practices that want to retain client control rather than making an outside referral.
  • Teams willing to use standardized intake, scope, conflict, and security procedures.

Professional and evidentiary limits

  • White-labeling cannot obscure authorship where law, ethics, contract, or the end client requires disclosure.
  • Medisprudence does not contact or solicit the partner’s client outside the agreed workflow.
  • Wholesale pricing depends on predictable volume and standardized scope; complex work may consume multiple credits.
  • No engagement bypasses conflict screening, professional boundaries, or data-security requirements.
Practical questions

Before the engagement begins

Does the physician’s name appear on the report?

That is agreed in advance. Some engagements require disclosed authorship; others permit partner-branded consulting work with an accurate description of the physician-review layer.

Can the LNC firm edit the report?

Formatting and client-facing presentation can be coordinated. Clinical conclusions, source meaning, limitations, and authorship must not be altered in a misleading way.

Are retainers required?

No for initial ad hoc matters. Retainers are appropriate after both parties validate fit, scope consistency, communication, and volume.

How are conflicts handled?

Every matter is screened from non-PHI case facts before records are accepted. Medisprudence does not serve both sides of the same matter, and any related-party or prior-engagement concern is resolved before scope is confirmed.

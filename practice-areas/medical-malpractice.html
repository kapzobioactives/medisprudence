Practice Area

Medical Malpractice

Standard-of-care documentation analysis and pre-expert case intelligence for plaintiff and defense med-mal teams. The most expensive mistake in med-mal litigation is engaging the wrong expert before the record is ready.

Founder's Advantage in Med-Mal

Standard of care documentation is the core of every med-mal case. Dr. Kasturi's background in utilization management — where clinical necessity is evaluated against documented care standards — gives him a precise lens for identifying where the documentation record will be challenged by defense experts. In med-mal cases, the question is not just whether the standard of care was met, but whether the record adequately documents the clinical reasoning, the alternatives considered, the informed consent process, and the departure from expected practice. Medisprudence identifies where that documentation is strong and where it creates the vulnerability the defense expert will exploit.

Case types we analyze

Surgical errors

Wrong-level surgery, nerve injury, retained instrument, anastomotic failure — documentation of surgical plan, informed consent, and post-operative management.

Failure to diagnose

Cancer, PE, MI, aortic dissection, appendicitis — documentation of differential diagnosis, workup ordered, and risk factor assessment.

Anesthesia events

Epidural complications, awareness under anesthesia, airway management failures — the most documentation-intensive med-mal cases.

OB/Birth injury

Cerebral palsy, Erb's palsy, shoulder dystocia, C-section timing — fetal monitoring strip interpretation and documentation of departure from protocol.

Medication errors

Dosing errors, drug interactions, contraindication failures — pharmacy records, ordering documentation, and provider communication.

Nursing home neglect

Pressure ulcer development, fall documentation, medication administration records, staffing ratios — regulatory standard documentation.

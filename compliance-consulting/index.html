Compliance Consulting

Physician-authored clinical analysis for ERISA benefits attorneys and plan compliance officers — the same payer-side UM credential applied to plan-level regulatory exposure instead of individual claim disputes.

The credential basis

Prior payer-side review of medical and behavioral-health claims informs the clinical comparison of utilization-management criteria and documentation. That experience does not replace benefits counsel, actuarial or network analysis, or current legal verification. Medisprudence does not use confidential or proprietary information from any former employer.

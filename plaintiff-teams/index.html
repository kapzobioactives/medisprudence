For Plaintiff Teams

Physician case intelligence for plaintiff litigation

IME deconstruction, case viability screening, Defense Medical Lens pre-mediation analysis, and full intelligence reports — from a physician who has performed the same reviews that defense teams commission.

Why plaintiff teams choose Medisprudence

Our founding physician served as a payer-side utilization-review physician — evaluating the same types of claims that defense IME physicians evaluate. He knows how defense medical reviews are constructed, where they structurally fail, and what documentation patterns make a plaintiff record defensible. When Medisprudence deconstructs an IME, maps treatment gaps, or projects defense pressure points, that analysis is grounded in operational knowledge of the review methodology.

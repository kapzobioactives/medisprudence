The Medisprudence Difference

Our physician has applied InterQual and MCG criteria to 3,000+ payer-side US claim reviews — and serves both plaintiff and defense under conflict-screened, separate engagements. The result is bilateral medical analysis calibrated to how claims are actually reviewed, not generic record summarization.

Our founding physician served as a utilization management physician for a major US commercial payer — applying InterQual and MCG criteria to real claims and understanding exactly how medical reviews are constructed. That operational knowledge applies identically whether helping a plaintiff attorney deconstruct a defense IME or helping a defense team ensure their own IME will survive cross-examination. Medisprudence follows the analysis, not a side — and the fact that both sides are willing to pay for it is the strongest signal of its objectivity.

MHPAEA Behavioral Health Parity Review

ERISA Benefits Attorneys · Plan Compliance Officers

Physician-authored comparative analysis of a health plan's behavioral health UM criteria against its medical/surgical criteria across all six NQTL categories. Identifies where BH criteria are more restrictive than analogous M/S criteria — the exact disparity DOL and HHS are enforcing since CAA 2021 and the 2024 MHPAEA final rule.

Deliverable includes
  • — Side-by-side NQTL comparison table, all six categories
  • — Physician narrative with citation (29 CFR §2590.712)
  • — Risk grading: Compliant / Borderline / Exposed
  • — Physician credential block and engagement disclaimer
$3,000 – $8,000 · 10–15 business days

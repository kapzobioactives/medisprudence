Physician Medical Record & IME Report Review for Litigation

Medisprudence provides independent physician review of medical records, IME reports, and insurance claim files for plaintiff attorneys, defense counsel, insurance carriers, and ERISA litigators across the United States. Every analysis is authored by a physician with more than 3,000 payer-side utilization review decisions under InterQual and MCG criteria — the same clinical standards insurers use to approve, deny, and defend coverage claims.

Services include IME deconstruction and rebuttal support, case viability screening, medical chronology preparation, defense medical record review, mental health parity review under MHPAEA, clinical denial pattern analysis, and medical reserve analysis for casualty carriers. Each deliverable is conflict-screened, physician-authored, and structured for demand packages, depositions, or trial preparation.

The clinical documentation inside a medical record either supports a claim or undermines it. Medisprudence identifies which — before opposing counsel does.

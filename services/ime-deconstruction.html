Sample Finding — Lumbar Spine · Fictional Clinical Data

Internal contradiction: examination vs. conclusion

IME Examination Findings (p.7)

"Positive straight leg raise at 40°. Diminished left ankle reflex. Antalgic gait on ambulation."

IME Conclusion (p.12)

"Symptoms are primarily subjective with limited objective findings. Return to full duty."

Medisprudence Analysis

The physician's own examination produced three objective findings (positive SLR, diminished reflex, antalgic gait), then characterized the presentation as "primarily subjective with limited objective findings." The internal contradiction between page 7 and page 12 is disposable on deposition with a single question.

Deposition Question

"You documented a positive straight leg raise at 40 degrees in your examination. Is a positive straight leg raise an objective or subjective finding?"

## Architecture changes
- Pre-suit demand support added as a limited three-matter firm pilot, not a new top-level page
- Rescission and contestability added as a medical-record module within existing insurance services
- Workers compensation narrowed to transferable medical-evidence modules; independent impairment ratings, MMI certification, legal apportionment, and fitness-for-duty are excluded
- Mass-tort work made pilot-first with a hard monthly capacity requirement
- Package discounts removed; value is one scope, one approval, one transfer, and one invoice
- Priority review language standardized as conditional, from +50%, never guaranteed
- $350 screening preserved as a tightly defined entry product; substantive new-client work generally begins at $750
